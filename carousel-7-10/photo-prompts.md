# Higgsfield prompts, carousel "ההקפה השביעית"

Shared style, appended to every prompt:
`quiet symbolic still life, low key lighting, deep shadows, muted desaturated tones, 35mm film grain, shallow depth of field, no text, no logos, no identifiable faces, vertical 4:5`

Rule: symbolic only. Nothing that looks like documentation of 7.10 (no soldiers in action, no burning houses, no real places from the attack).

1. A Torah scroll in its velvet mantle resting alone on a synagogue bimah, dim light through a high window, empty pews behind.
2. A circle of empty wooden chairs in a dark synagogue hall, one chair pulled slightly out of the circle.
3. A window with cracked glass at night, a child's drawing still taped to the inside of the glass.
4. A holiday dinner table set for a family, candles lit, two place settings untouched with folded napkins.
5. A foggy two lane road at dawn, headlights of a single car approaching from far away.
6. A lone car parked on an empty desert road at night, door open, interior light on.
7. An Israeli flag moving in the wind above the stone walls of the Old City of Jerusalem at blue hour, seen from far away.
8. Silhouette from behind of a father holding a small daughter on his shoulders, looking at a sunrise, faces not visible.
9. Rows of memorial candles burning in glass cups on a stone ledge at night.
